## Current Visual Observations & Problems
1. **Flat Background and Sky**: The background image has a sky gradient but feels static and simple. Weather overlays (rain, snow, lightning) are present but lack deep visual ambiance (e.g., fog/mist depth, light beam ray effects, warm golden hour tones).
2. **Floating Tree visual**: The tree is rendered as an image overlaying the backdrop. It lacks grounding elements like a customized seasonal base glow ("tree stage halo"), shadows on the ground, or surrounding grass/vegetation layers that evolve with the growth stage.
3. **Basic Particle Styling**: Particles (motes, rain streaks, snow, butterflies) are generated, but they lack distinct seasonal/weather tuning (like distinct velocity, opacity, and path changes between snowy/winter and stormy/heavy-rain states).
4. **Action Burst Feedback**: The action bursts are simply static circles that pop up in a fixed "+XP" format rather than matching the specific tool used (e.g. splash for watering, charge for storm) and are not positioned near their actual interaction targets (e.g., canopy, roots).
5. **UI & Panels Styling**: The dashboard and action panels feel a bit cluttered and blocky. They lack premium frosted glass card styling (`backdrop-filter`) and smooth transitions, especially on mobile viewport widths where buttons can feel tight.
